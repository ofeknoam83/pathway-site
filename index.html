The problem

Information was never the problem. Finishing is.

Every rule is online. What sinks people is the gate they didn't know to check, the office that never replies, and the rule that changed in month nine.

50–200hspent on forums, embassy sites and group chats

And still no one asked the two questions that decided the case: which passport, which diploma.

€2k–8kfor an agency to do the chasing

Relocation firms and consultants do the work, at a price most individuals can't justify, and only for one country.

Renewals, hour caps, address changes, fee updates. A chatbot answers once. A case manager stays.

Trust

What the agent does on its own, and what it never does.

You set a mandate for who it may write to on your behalf. Everything else reaches you as a one-tap approval card first.

Within your mandate

  • Write to schools, landlords, employers and archives in your name, and chase them for weeks
  • Request certificates, apostilles and sworn translations in the right order
  • Pre-fill portal forms from your documents, then stop at the review screen
  • Watch appointment slots, deadlines and rule changes, and re-plan

Never, under any setting

  • Pay, sign, or submit anything to a government body
  • Type your passport or identity numbers into any form
  • Give a legal opinion: regulated judgement goes to a licensed adviser, named on your case
  • Misrepresent your situation to improve your odds

Questions

Fair questions.

Is this legal advice?

No. Pathway shows you the official rule, with its source and the date we last checked it, and applies it to the facts you give us. Where a legal opinion is required or the law regulates who may advise, a licensed adviser is brought onto your case and named. You always see which steps were theirs.

What does the agent actually do without me?

Only what you allow in your mandate: writing to the kinds of counterparties you tick (schools, landlords, employers, archives), chasing them, requesting documents, pre-filling forms, and watching slots and rules. Payments, government submissions, signatures and anything that fixes a date always come to you first.

Which countries first?

Portugal and Spain for remote workers; Germany for skilled workers and families; Italy and Poland by descent; France and Germany student routes. Each corridor launches only after a licensed partner has reviewed its rules. Tell us your goal even if it isn't listed; demand decides the order.

What happens to my documents?

They're stored in the EU, encrypted with a key tied to your account, and read field by field only by the agents working your case. Nothing is used to train models. You can delete anything at any time.

What if my application is refused?

The plan carries a pinned fallback from day one, chosen so it shares most of the paperwork. On a refusal the agent stops, explains the reason as stated by the authority, and re-plans with you. It never resubmits on its own.

Why early access and not a product today?

Because we want to run real cases with a small group before opening up, and because every corridor needs rules verified by a licensed partner first. The first 200 cases get the attention of a concierge and a price that stays fixed for the life of the case.
